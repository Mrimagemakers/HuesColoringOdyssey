The crew & level ups

Hue never rolls solo.

Brush and Dot are locked at first. Solve the optional side puzzles to unlock them, then summon them into any fight. Every enemy erased, wall tagged and level cleared also earns XP, so Hue levels up from 1 to 10 and eventually grows wings.

BRUSH

The blue bot with the giant paintbrush. Unlock him with the Color Code puzzle in Level 1. Press 1 to summon him: he fires paint on the beat, so his hits even count against bosses. Press 1 again for a Rainbow Barrage.

DOT

The yellow bot with a TV for a face. Unlock him with the Subway Lights puzzle in Level 2. Press 2 to summon him: he stuns nearby enemies, pulls in pickups and heals you. Press 2 again for a Screen Flash that stuns everything around you.

Paint anything. Tag with it.

Step into Hue's studio and paint on the easel or straight into the air around you in 3D. Hang your best work on the gallery wall. Set a painting as your tag, and Hue sprays it on every tag wall in the game.

Where the game comes from

Start with the book.

The game grows out of Darnell Clark's coloring book, Hue's Got Flow: A Hip-Hop Coloring Odyssey. It's full of subway cars, mic-in-hand MCs and boombox crews waiting for your color. Hover over or tap a page to color it in.

The story

Creativity is under attack.

Hue Davis is a New York street artist and underground rapper. In an abandoned subway station he finds a glowing Golden Pen that can create anything he imagines.

One stroke opens a portal into the Coloring Odyssey, an unfinished world of floating buildings, graffiti clouds and musical rivers. Shadows are draining it of its color and its beat.

Every wall Hue tags and every beat he lands brings a piece of the world back. When the pen is fully mastered, the story says he hands it to a young artist, and another portal opens.

The Golden Pen

Hue's power source. It restores color, remixes the world and opens portals.

Ink Blots

Hopping puddles of doubt that leak into the streets. Three Ink Shots erase one.

Creative Block Monsters

Heavy stone doubts topped with erasers. They slam the ground, so keep moving.

Ink Shadows

Flying wraiths that drain color from the Odyssey. Hit them on the beat.

The Eraser King

The final boss. He wants a blank page. Coming in level 12.
